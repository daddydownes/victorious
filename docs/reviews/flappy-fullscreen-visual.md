# Flappy V fullscreen visual review

Reviewed September 6, 2026 in native desktop Chrome at `http://127.0.0.1:8938/demo`.
This was the fifth sequential Sol review pass by the same specialist, not five
separate Sol agents.

The game fills the browser content area and keeps the score, exit control, tier
label and progress outside the flight line. The animated black field, restrained
gold motes and floor perspective retain the original site's atmosphere. The
unchanged V-and-star silhouette stays legible against it.

The pillar study showed convincing depth: broad black-metal faces, dark side
returns, inset horizontal seams and substantial brass passage caps. The arch
study was an obvious change to floating flared rails. The slalom study was also
immediately distinct, using long opposing diagonal rails whose bevel followed
the collision edge.

The final jaw study was revised after the user requested smooth, matching forms
without harsh points. Its paired blunt cosine lobes now read as heavy opposing
closing jaws while retaining the same polished rail material. The solid outer
backs give them more mass than the slalom rails. Their widened neutral opening
preserves the authored minimum clearance, and the rounded gold joins follow the
same sampled collision outline.

A live keyboard check started a run, paused it with `P`, displayed the native
paused panel and Resume controls, and resumed from the visible button. No saved
reward state was cleared; the demo uses isolated storage.
